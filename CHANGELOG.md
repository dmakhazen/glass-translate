# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- Transparent frame that translates text under it and draws the translation over the original (OCR with PaddleOCR PP-OCRv5).
- Engines without keys: Google, Bing, MyMemory; optional local Ollama.
- Auto source language, 22 target languages, interface in 10 languages.
- Auto-translate every 1–10 s or on a button; show original; always on top.
- Onboarding tour, help, donate links. Portable build.
